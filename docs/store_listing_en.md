## Full description
**SitaRam** is a respectful study companion for readers exploring the Valmiki Ramayana and the seven traditional Kandas.

The app is being built around a source-first principle: imported material is not automatically presented as verified scripture, and AI answers are not allowed to rely on unapproved passages. SitaRam shows corpus coverage transparently so readers can distinguish available material from content that is still awaiting verification.

### Study features

- **Seven-Kanda navigation** — Bala, Ayodhya, Aranya, Kishkindha, Sundara, Yuddha, and Uttara Kanda structure
- **Multilingual interface** — English, বাংলা (Bangla), and Español (Spanish)
- **Corpus coverage status** — see how much material has been imported and human-verified
- **AI Guide** — educational question-and-answer assistance with explicit limitations
- **Citation safeguards** — approved source passages are required before trusted citations are shown
- **No-evidence behavior** — when approved evidence is unavailable, the AI Guide says so instead of inventing scripture
- **Feedback controls** — report inaccurate citations, translation concerns, or ungrounded AI responses
- **Local preferences** — language and app preferences are stored on your device

### Responsible AI

SitaRam AI is for **learning, study, and reflection**. It is not a religious authority and does not provide divine rulings or guaranteed spiritual interpretations.

Questions submitted to the AI Guide are sent over the internet to the SitaRam backend for processing. Depending on the production configuration, hosting and AI service providers may process request content to provide the feature. Please do not include sensitive personal information in AI questions or feedback.

### Source transparency

SitaRam uses a verification workflow for source provenance, Kanda/Sarga numbering, placeholder detection, human review, and AI retrieval eligibility. The app does **not** claim that its Ramayana corpus is complete or fully verified unless the generated coverage report supports that claim.

### Privacy

- No account is required for the current app experience
- The Android app requests internet access for online AI features
- App preferences are stored locally
- AI questions and submitted feedback are transmitted to the SitaRam backend when you use those features
- SitaRam does not sell user data or use advertising SDKs in the current release

Please review the published Privacy Policy before using online AI features.
