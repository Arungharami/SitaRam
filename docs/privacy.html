1. Overview

SitaRam is a Ramayana study application with local app preferences and optional online AI and feedback features. SitaRam does not require an account for the current app experience and does not use advertising SDKs in the current release.

2. Information stored on your device

The app may store settings and preferences locally on your device, such as language selection and other app-state preferences required for the experience.

3. AI Guide data

When you use the AI Guide, the question you enter and technical request information needed to process the request are transmitted over HTTPS to the SitaRam backend.

The backend may be hosted by a third-party infrastructure provider and may use an AI model or AI service configured by the developer. Those providers may process request content as necessary to provide the AI feature.

Do not submit passwords, financial information, government identifiers, health records, or other sensitive personal information in AI questions.

4. Feedback and reports

If you submit AI feedback or report a response, the selected rating or reason and any comment you choose to provide are transmitted to the SitaRam backend so the report can be reviewed and used to improve safety and accuracy.

5. Permissions

The current Android release requests internet access so online AI and feedback features can communicate with the SitaRam backend.

The current release does not request access to your precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, or advertising identifier through the Android manifest.

6. Data use

  • Provide the requested AI response.
  • Validate and improve answer quality and citation safety.
  • Process user feedback and safety reports.
  • Maintain security, reliability, and abuse prevention for the service.

SitaRam does not sell user data.

7. Third-party services

Production deployments may rely on third-party hosting and AI infrastructure, including Hugging Face or other providers selected by the developer. Their handling of data is also governed by their applicable terms and privacy policies.

Google Play also processes information related to app distribution, installation, and Play services under Google's policies.

8. Data retention and security

SitaRam aims to minimize server-side collection and uses HTTPS for production AI requests. Operational infrastructure may retain limited logs needed for security, reliability, troubleshooting, or abuse prevention.

9. Children's privacy

Users should not submit personal information about children through AI questions or feedback. If the app is distributed to children or included in a child-directed audience, the developer must complete the applicable Google Play Families and privacy requirements before publication.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when app features, service providers, or data practices change. The date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.
